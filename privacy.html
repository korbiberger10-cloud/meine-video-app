Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

ClipForge is a personal tool operated by a single developer. It uploads the developer's own videos to the developer's own TikTok and YouTube accounts.

2. Data We Access

When connecting a TikTok account, we access only the following data via the official TikTok API: basic profile information (username, display name) and permission to upload videos on behalf of the account owner.

3. Data Storage

We do not store, collect, or share any personal data of third parties. Authentication tokens are stored securely and used solely for the intended upload functionality.

4. Third Parties

Video content is transmitted directly to TikTok and YouTube via their official APIs. No data is shared with any other third party.
